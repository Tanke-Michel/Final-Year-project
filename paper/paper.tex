\subsection{One quantizer for training, evaluation and deployment}
\TODO{This subsection carries the methodological weight of the paper. Three
points. (1) Quantization-aware training only transfers if the simulated
rounding matches the deployed rounding; GGUF Q4\_0 is not textbook symmetric
int4 --- the largest-magnitude value in each block of 32 keeps its sign and
maps to $-8$, so the step is $\max/8$ --- and a generic int4 fake quantizer
trains against a different grid. (2) A single reimplementation of llama.cpp's
Q4\_0/Q8\_0 arithmetic is therefore used for the straight-through-estimator
fake quantization during training, for rounding the weights before every
evaluation, and to specify the exported file; it is verified bit-identical
against the ggml C reference and against llama.cpp's own gguf-py
(\NUM{458,752} values, zero differences). (3) Every configuration is scored
in its deployed form, never in fp16, so the reported quality is the quality of
the model on the phone. State the one simplification: runtime 8-bit
activation rounding inside llama.cpp's matrix multiplies is not simulated.}
